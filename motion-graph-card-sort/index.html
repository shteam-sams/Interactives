Motion Graph Card Sort

Instructions
  • Sort the cards into categories. Seven columns are provided, but you can add or remove columns to use as many as you think you need.
  • Type a short description of each category in the box at the bottom of each column you use.
  • Some cards are repeated. This is intentional.
  • The “best” sort uses all 7 columns, where each column has 5 cards, one of each: position graph, velocity graph, acceleration graph, direction description, speed description.

Drag cards with your mouse or finger, or click a card and then click the column where it goes. Drop a card on top of another card to put it in front of that card. Your work saves automatically in this browser.

Hint: the 7 types of motion

Each finished column describes one of these seven motions. Your job is to figure out which graphs show each one.

  1. Moving forward and speeding up
  2. Moving forward and slowing down
  3. Moving forward at a constant speed
  4. Moving backward and speeding up
  5. Moving backward and slowing down
  6. Moving backward at a constant speed
  7. Not moving at all

Questions to ask yourself

  • Position graph: Is the position getting bigger, getting smaller, or staying the same? Is it changing faster and faster, slower and slower, or at a steady rate?
  • Velocity graph: What could it mean when the line is above zero? Below zero? What does it mean when the line moves toward zero or away from zero?
  • Acceleration graph: Is the velocity changing? If it is, is it going up or going down? (Careful: think about whether that always means “speeding up.”)

“Help me get started” puts the direction and speed cards into the 7 columns, one motion per column. Your graph cards stay where they are.
